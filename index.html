Leo Versteegen

About me

My name is Leo Versteegen, and I am a departmental research fellow in combinatorics at the London School of Economics and Poltical Science. Until Summer 2024, I did my PhD under the supervision of Julia Wolf at the University of Cambridge. Directly after that, I completed a five month LMS Early Career Research Fellowship during which I was hosted by Alexey Pokrovskiy at University College London. Please get in touch for a copy of my CV.
